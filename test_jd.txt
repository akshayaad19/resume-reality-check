Working at Margn offers exposure to real-world AI applications in highly dynamic, service-oriented industries. Team members collaborate across product, engineering, and client success functions to deliver scalable AI solutions tailored to each business. The organization values innovation, practical problem-solving, and ethical use of AI technologies.

You will work closely with clients to translate business workflows into robust AI capabilities, improve existing models based on performance metrics, and ensure reliability and scalability in a remote environment. This is a full-time remote role that involves regular collaboration through online tools, participation in code reviews, documentation of solutions, and continuous improvement of our AI platform.
